# Privacy Policy - GETS MODES TOOLKIT

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

GETS MODES TOOLKIT is an Android toolbox made by Anas. This page explains what the app does with your data.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cloud backend. The developer does not receive any of your data.

## What stays on your phone
Notes, templates, favorites, settings, converted images, PDFs, QR images and downloaded files are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. You choose when to share or export a file.

## Network use
The internet permission is used for two optional features only:
- **Video Downloader**: downloads a direct video file link that you type in.
- **Currency Converter**: requests public exchange rates from `open.er-api.com`. No personal information is sent; the request is a plain rates lookup.

All other tools work fully offline.

## Camera and third-party components
- **QR scanning with the camera** uses the Google code scanner from Google Play services. The camera view is provided by Google Play services, so the app itself does not need camera permission. Scanning from a picked image is done offline inside the app.
- **Text from Image (OCR)** uses Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The recognition runs on your phone.
Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to Google Play services.

## Your control
Delete the app or clear its storage in Android settings to remove all data stored by the app.
